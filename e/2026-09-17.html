🌐 Geopolitics & the Economy 2 items

Russia sanctions bill clears Congress with tariff powers aimed at Russian energy buyersCNBC / BBC

The House passed the Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026 by 262–159, sending it to Trump. It authorizes tariffs of up to 100% on the top five purchasers of Russian oil or gas. China and India are the largest buyers, and India is already facing a 100% tariff threat. An earlier 2025 draft had proposed 500%. Hungary is lobbying for an exemption, and Austria's Raiffeisen shares fell on allegations of sanctions-evasion channels.

Why it matters: if applied, the tariffs would force Indian refiners to replace discounted Urals crude, tightening an already stressed seaborne oil market.

Sources: CNBC · BBC — India

Ukraine: central bank hikes to 16%, growth forecast cutEkonomichna Pravda

The National Bank of Ukraine raised its policy rate from 15.5% to 16% as inflation runs above forecast, and cut its GDP growth outlook by roughly a third. The Finance Ministry deferred UAH 39bn of energy-resilience spending to December because of a funding gap. Russian strikes continue to hit industrial assets, including the fourth strike this month on the Zaporizhstal steel plant.

Why it matters: tighter money plus deferred energy investment raises the risk of winter supply stress and a larger external financing need.

Sources: Ekonomichna Pravda · Kyiv Independent
