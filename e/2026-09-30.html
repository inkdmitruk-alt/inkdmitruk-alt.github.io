Hormuz flows back to ~80% of pre-war levelsAGBI / Kpler via CNBC / Sunday Guardian

Oil and product flows through the Strait of Hormuz averaged 13.1 million b/d last week, according to Kpler, just under 80% of the pre-war 17.1 million b/d. Saudi Arabia has restored East-West pipeline flows to around 3.5 million b/d, allowing exports to bypass the strait via Yanbu. Qatar says it is running shuttle diplomacy between Washington and Tehran to find common ground, and Iran has received a US proposal following Qatar talks. The net effect is two-way volatility: physical supply is healing while political headlines swing the premium.

Why it matters: tanker rates, insurance costs and Gulf-dependent Asian refiners are the second-order exposures; a normalization of Hormuz would be disinflationary for the US and Europe.

🚢 Trade, Tariffs & Policy 3 items

Trump’s Section 301 global tariffs face the trade courtCNN / Foreign Policy / US News (Reuters)

A three-judge panel at the US Court of International Trade in Manhattan hears arguments today from four small businesses and 25 Democratic-led states challenging the tariffs imposed this summer under Section 301. The duties run 10–12.5% on nearly every trading partner — 59 countries plus the EU — on the stated grounds that they fail to police forced labor in supply chains. Plaintiffs argue the administration is effectively reimposing the worldwide tariffs that courts previously struck down.

It is the third time in under two years that courts have been asked to rule on sweeping import tariffs. A ruling against the government would raise the prospect of refunds and lower landed costs for importers.

Why it matters: retailers, consumer-goods importers and autos are the most exposed; a loss for the administration would also force it to look for yet another statutory basis, extending trade-policy uncertainty.

Sources: CNN — Trump’s tariffs are back in court · Foreign Policy — Section 301 forced labor tariffs challenge · US News — Trade court to weigh challenge

Europe squeezes Russia’s shadow fleet at seaDefense News / IISS / USNI News
