🎙 Trump & Policy Signals 3 items

Diesel deal with Putin: Treasury license through April 2027, volumes up to ~4.8m tonnesAP via MPR / NPR / CNBC / Axios

Trump said Putin agreed in a phone call to supply more than 300,000 tonnes of diesel immediately, 500,000 tonnes in November, 1 million tonnes after that and another 3 million tonnes 'within a short period'. Treasury immediately issued a temporary license: sanctions will not apply until April 2027 to Russian diesel loaded onto tankers as of Friday — the first diesel waiver longer than the standard 30 days since February 2022.

The White House did not say who pays or when cargoes arrive; Russia has banned diesel exports since July and the IEA estimates Russian output down ~30% after Ukrainian refinery strikes. CNBC reports critics say the move contradicts the sanctions law Trump signed last month (shadow-fleet sanctions, tariffs up to 100% on top buyers of Russian oil). Diesel futures dipped; analyst Clayton Seigle expects little price impact but 'great benefit to Russia'.

Why it matters: it signals the administration will trade sanctions leverage for pump prices before Nov 3 — and casts doubt on enforcement of secondary tariffs on Russian-oil buyers like India and China.

Sources: AP via MPR News · NPR · CNBC · Bloomberg
