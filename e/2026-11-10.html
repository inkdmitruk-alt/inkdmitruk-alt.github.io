October CPI +0.4% m/m vs 0.3% expected; core +0.3% vs 0.2%CNBC / BLS

Headline consumer prices rose 0.4% in October after 0.6% in September; core rose 0.3% for a second month. BLS published no year-over-year rate because the 2025 government shutdown prevented October 2025 data collection. Shelter rose 0.3% and accounted for over 30% of the headline gain. Energy rose 0.7% with gasoline +1%, while electricity jumped 0.9% — which CNBC links to AI data-center demand.

Food rose 0.5% (beef +0.9%, snacks +1%), transportation services 0.8%, while used cars fell 1.2% and tariff-sensitive apparel rose only 0.1%. Federated Hermes' Karen Manna called it 'the report the market could least afford to see.'

Why it matters: the composition is sticky — shelter, services and power, not one-off goods — which is exactly what makes a central bank hike rather than wait.

Sources: CNBC
