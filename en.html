October CPI: +0.4% m/m headline, +0.3% core for a third straight monthCNBC / Morningstar

Consumer prices rose 0.4% in October against a 0.3% consensus, and core rose 0.3% against 0.2%, according to the BLS report released Nov 10. Food and rent drove the overshoot. The BLS did not publish a 12-month rate because of last year's shutdown gap; economists estimate year-over-year inflation accelerated toward ~4% from 3.7% in September. Three consecutive 0.3% core prints meet the threshold Governor Waller had set for considering a 50bp December hike. Dallas Fed President Lorie Logan says she sees a need for two more quarter-point increases.

Why it matters: the Fed is now in a hiking cycle with the policy rate at 3.75–4%. The annual CPI figure returns only on Dec 10, after the Dec 8–9 FOMC decision, so the Fed must act on monthly data.

Sources: CNBC · Morningstar
